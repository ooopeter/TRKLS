Nimzy — Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Nimzy creates parallax animations, wigglegrams, GIFs, and video from image sequences. It collects no data.

Data Collection

Nimzy collects, stores, and transmits no personal data. It contains no analytics, tracking, accounts, or advertising.

Files

All processing and export is performed locally. Images, projects, and exported files remain on the device and are not transmitted.

Network

Nimzy requires no internet connection and transmits no data. One outbound connection is possible: selecting the website link in the credits opens that page in the default browser.
